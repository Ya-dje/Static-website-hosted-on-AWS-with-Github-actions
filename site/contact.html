FAQs

Common questions

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! We ship to 15+ countries across Africa, Europe, and the Middle East. Shipping times vary by destination, typically 5–14 business days.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery for unworn, unwashed items in original packaging. Exchanges are always free.

Can I place a custom or bulk order?

Absolutely. We welcome custom and wholesale orders. Please use the contact form above and select "Custom Order" or "Wholesale / B2B".

How do I find my size?

Each product page includes a detailed size guide. If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up. Our team is also happy to advise.
