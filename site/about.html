How it began

A vision for
modern elegance.

Olive's Space was founded in 2019 in Yaoundé, Cameroon, by a team of passionate fashion designers who believed that every woman deserves to feel extraordinary — regardless of where she is in the world.

What began as a small boutique with a handful of handpicked pieces has grown into a beloved brand serving thousands of clients across 15+ countries. Our philosophy has always been simple: quality, elegance, and authenticity.

Every piece in our collection is carefully selected or designed in-house, with meticulous attention to fabric, fit, and finish. We believe fashion should make you feel powerful, confident, and entirely yourself.
